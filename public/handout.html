🎪 Educator Deployment Guide

Teacher Quick-Start & Directions

How to share with colleagues, assign to students, and collect grades

🔗 1. Which App Link to Share

Share this link with students and other grade-level teachers. It opens on district Chromebooks with no iframe or Google AI Studio login:

https://arosenbaum993.github.io/TypeCarnival/

Don't share the old AI Studio preview link (it starts with ais-pre-). District filters block it.

📢 2. Ready-to-Post Google Classroom Announcement

Copy and paste this message into your Google Classroom assignment or announcement stream:

🎪 WELCOME TO TYPECARNIVAL! 🎪 Hey class! Today we are practicing typing with TypeCarnival, an arcade carnival keyboarding game! 👉 HOW TO BEGIN: 1. Click our class link: https://arosenbaum993.github.io/TypeCarnival/ 2. Choose your name from the menu at the top of the screen. 3. Head into the Midway tents starting with Tent 1: Home Row Anchor (A S D F and J K L ;) 4. Earn Carnival Tickets to play arcade games and win prizes in the Prize Booth! 🎯 Golden Rule: Keep your fingers resting on the Home Row (A S D F and J K L ;) and aim for 90%+ accuracy!

📊 3. Tracking Student Growth

  • Add your roster first: Click "Teacher View," then "Add Student."
  • Teacher Command Center: Click "Teacher View" in the top bar to see each student's WPM, accuracy %, struggle keys, and daily practice streak.
  • Gradebook CSV Export: Click "Download CSV" in Teacher Command to export class records for your gradebook or report cards.

Important: Progress saves in the browser on each Chromebook. Students should use the same assigned Chromebook every session, and you should export the CSV from that device. Clearing browser data erases progress.
